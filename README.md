# CasaNova Design

Responsive storefront prototype based on the supplied page designs. Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve this directory with `python -m http.server` and visit `http://localhost:8000`.

The seven pages share `style.css` and `script.js`. Product data, reviews, contact details, prices, and other business information are illustrative content from the mockups; verify them before publishing. The images in `assets/` are cropped from the supplied screenshots and should be replaced with original assets when available.

The catalog search, category/price filters, review filters and product gallery work in the browser with sample data. The contact form and cart are visual demonstrations only: they do not send messages or create orders. Account, favorites, checkout, payment, and database/PHP integration are not implemented. Fonts use Google Fonts with system fallbacks.
